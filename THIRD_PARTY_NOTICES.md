# Third-party notices and credits

JKXR Diorama MR is a modified descendant of
[Team Beef's JKXR](https://github.com/Team-Beef-Studios/JKXR), which is based on
[OpenJK](https://github.com/JACoders/OpenJK) and the Jedi Academy/Jedi Outcast
engine source released by Raven Software. The Git history is intentionally
preserved so original authorship and later modifications remain traceable.

## Packaged VR and mod assets

The repository inherits JKXR VR weapon and support packages used by the port.
Their detailed author credits are retained in
[assets/packaged_mods_credits.txt](assets/packaged_mods_credits.txt) and in the
in-game credits. They include work credited to Elin and Vince Crusty, and
GGDynamicWeapons credited to Grab.

These art and mod assets remain copyright their respective authors. Their
inclusion does not relicense them as GPL-2.0 source code; reuse or redistribution
must follow the original authors' applicable terms and notices.

## Commercial game data

This repository and its releases do not include the commercial Jedi Academy
game archives. Users must provide data from a legally purchased copy of the
game. Generated APKs, installers, local saves, device logs, and release signing
keys are not source-controlled.
